# Code of conduct

This applies to every Solarity-AI repository, issue, pull request and review, and to anyone who takes part.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique the work, not the person.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and take feedback the same way.
- Keep discussions on topic, and keep private information private.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, intimidation, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to [ata.turhan@solarityai.com](mailto:ata.turhan@solarityai.com). Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter is protected from retaliation. Breaking this code can lead to a warning, removal of content, or loss of access.
