# Security policy

This policy applies to every Solarity-AI repository that doesn't have its own `SECURITY.md`. It also covers solarityai.com, the Solarity AI apps (Looks Max, IQ Max, Sleep Max, and Fitness Max, listed as Only Fitness), and the SaaS products (Engineering Max, Management Max, and Marketing Max).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately. **Don't open a public issue.**

- **Preferred:** use **Report a vulnerability** on the affected repository's **Security** tab
- **Email:** [ata.turhan@solarityai.com](mailto:ata.turhan@solarityai.com) with the subject `Security report`

Include the affected product, URL, or file, the steps to reproduce, and the impact.

## What to expect

- We'll confirm we received your report within **3 business days**.
- We'll keep you updated until the issue is resolved.
- If you'd like, we'll credit you once a fix ships.

## Out of scope

- Reports from automated scanners with no demonstrated impact
- Social engineering, physical attacks, and denial-of-service testing
