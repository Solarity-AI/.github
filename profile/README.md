# Solarity AI

**Building for human potential.** Solarity AI builds AI self-improvement apps and the tools that let its small team ship them fast. Dallas, Texas.

## Apps (iOS and Android)

- [Looks Max](https://solarityai.com/apps/looks-max/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile&utm_campaign=profile): appearance and self-care
- [IQ Max](https://solarityai.com/apps/iq-max/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile&utm_campaign=profile): cognitive practice
- [Sleep Max](https://solarityai.com/apps/sleep-max/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile&utm_campaign=profile): sleep and daily routines
- [Fitness Max](https://solarityai.com/apps/fitness-max/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile&utm_campaign=profile) (listed as Only Fitness): fitness and nutrition

## SaaS products (early access)

- [Engineering Max](https://engineeringmax.solarityai.com/): find and fix what keeps a mobile app out of the App Store
- [Management Max](https://managementmax.solarityai.com/): governed weekly metrics for mobile app companies
- [Marketing Max](https://marketingmax.solarityai.com/): your next Reel, made from one that already worked

## Free tools (public repos)

- [ai-usage-taskbar-widgets](https://github.com/Solarity-AI/ai-usage-taskbar-widgets): see how much of your weekly Claude and Codex allowance is used, on the Windows taskbar or the macOS menu bar
- [hw-widget](https://github.com/Solarity-AI/hw-widget): temperature, CPU, memory and GPU at a glance on Windows, GNOME and macOS
- [ci-widget](https://github.com/Solarity-AI/ci-widget): every running GitHub Actions job on the Windows taskbar, grouped by machine, plus the queue
- [llm-iq-index](https://github.com/Solarity-AI/llm-iq-index): estimated IQ scores for the top closed and open-weight LLMs ([live site](https://solarity-ai.github.io/llm-iq-index/))
- [claude-enter-newline](https://github.com/Solarity-AI/claude-enter-newline): Enter adds a new line and Ctrl/⌘+Enter sends, in the Claude desktop app and AI chats in Chrome
- [public-resources](https://github.com/Solarity-AI/public-resources): brand assets, press kit and one-pagers
