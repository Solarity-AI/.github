# Solarity AI

**Building for human potential.** Solarity AI builds AI self-improvement apps and the tools that let its small team ship them fast. Dallas, Texas.

## Apps (iOS and Android)

- [Looks Max](https://solarityai.com/apps/looks-max/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile&utm_campaign=profile): appearance and self-care
- [IQ Max](https://solarityai.com/apps/iq-max/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile&utm_campaign=profile): cognitive practice
- [Sleep Max](https://solarityai.com/apps/sleep-max/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile&utm_campaign=profile): sleep and daily routines
- [Fitness Max](https://solarityai.com/apps/fitness-max/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org-profile&utm_campaign=profile) (listed as Only Fitness): fitness and nutrition
